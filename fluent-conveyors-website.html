About the client

Fluent designs and manufactures custom conveyors in the USA for recycling, waste management and other heavy industries, and supplies the belts and parts that keep them running.

The website is where most of those relationships start. It has to explain a complex, made-to-order product, earn the trust of buyers spending six figures, and hand them to the right next step. Existing customers then move into the self-serve portal we also designed.

The challenge

A six-figure, made-to-order machine doesn’t sell from a spec sheet.

Heavy-duty conveyors start from $125,000 and are configured for each site. Buyers need to understand what fits their material and layout before they will talk to sales, and the site has to serve more than one kind of visitor.

Who the site has to work for
01Plant buyers and engineers comparing conveyor types and what can be customised for their site.
02Maintenance teams who need replacement belts and parts quickly, often for equipment already running.
03Dealers and prospective dealers who sell, install and service Fluent equipment regionally.
04Existing customers who need a quick route into their account and support.
The solution

Show the machine, explain the options, ask for one thing.

A dark, engineered look built on Fluent’s brand, with every page structured around what a buyer needs to decide, and a quote request always one click away.

01 · Homepage

Lead with the machine and one next step.

The homepage opens on Fluent equipment working in a real scrapyard, states what Fluent makes in one line, and puts “Request a quote” front and centre, followed by a “Trusted by” section of customers.

  • Video hero and a single primary action
  • Request a quote and Locate a dealer in the header on every page
02 · Conveyor models

Model pages that sell a $125,000 machine.

Each conveyor type gets its own page: what it is built for, the materials it handles and a starting price, so buyers arrive at a sales conversation already knowing which model fits.

  • Breadcrumbs from category to model
  • Starting prices shown up front
03 · Customisation

Make “fully custom” concrete.

Instead of a vague promise, the site lays out every way a conveyor can be tailored, so engineers can see their requirements are covered before they ask.

  • 14 options: safety, layout, belt and chain, size, drives, controls and more
  • Browsable as a carousel of short, scannable cards
04 · Belts & parts

The aftermarket, one click from a quote.

Replacement belts and parts have their own section for Fluent and other OEM equipment, so maintenance teams can find a roller chain, hinged steel, cleated or Z-pan belt and request it without hunting through the catalogue.

  • Belts and parts organised by type
  • Request a quote straight from each page
Built to grow sales

A site structured around how industrial buyers search.

The information architecture gives each audience its own way in, so the same site works for a first-time buyer, a maintenance lead and a dealer.

01By industry and application. Pages for recycling (MSW, OCC, paper, single-stream), waste management, transfer stations, municipalities and manufacturing, plus applications like baler infeed and sorting.
02By product. Conveyors, replacement belts, parts and extras, each with their own catalogue.
03Through dealers. A dealer locator for buyers who want local sales and service, and a programme page to recruit new dealers.
04For learning. Guides, a help centre, FAQs, a blog and a podcast that build trust before the first call.
The outcome

One front door for Fluent’s whole business.

The website and the customer portal work as one system: the site wins and qualifies new business, and the portal keeps those customers buying parts and service afterwards.

Qualified quote requests

Buyers reach the quote form already knowing the conveyor type and options they need, which shortens the sales conversation.

Dealer network

Buyers find local sales and service in a few clicks, and prospective dealers have a clear route to join.

Belts & parts revenue

Maintenance teams can find and request replacement belts and parts directly, keeping that demand with Fluent.
