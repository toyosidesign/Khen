How we work

We don’t start with screens.

We start with the people who use your product every day. What they do, what slows them down, and what they do instead. Then we design and build from there.

Step 1 · Observe
We watch the work happen.

We sit with your team and watch how they really use the product. The shortcuts, the spreadsheets on the side, the steps everyone skips.
