About the client

Fluent Conveyors supplies and services conveyor systems for customers who run several lines at once.

Quotes, parts orders, shipments, maintenance work orders and support tickets were spread across email, spreadsheets and separate tools. Customers had no single place to see what was happening on their lines.

The challenge

Six conveyor lines, five places to check.

Finding out whether a part had shipped or a maintenance task was overdue meant emailing someone at Fluent and waiting for an answer.

What the client needed
01One place for every quote, order and invoice.
02See late and upcoming maintenance before a line goes down.
03Track parts shipments all the way to site.
04Raise and follow support tickets without email.
The solution

One dashboard for every conveyor line.

Time with Fluent’s customers and support team first, then design and build in short releases.

01

Start the day with what needs attention.

Open quotes, shipments, maintenance and tickets are summarised at the top. Late and due work orders are listed first, so nothing slips.

02

Track every shipment to site.

Each order shows where it is, when it will arrive and which site it is going to, without a phone call.

Screen · Shipments in transit
03

Maintenance and help desk in one place.

Work orders, assets and support tickets share one view, so customers can see the full history of a line and raise a ticket in seconds.
