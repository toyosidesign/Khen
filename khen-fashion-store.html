About the store

Khen Fashion Store sells everyday clothing, bags and shoes online, with new arrivals every season and a catalogue that changes week to week.

The homepage carries most of the selling. It has to launch each new collection, make a broad catalogue feel easy to browse, and surface the products people are buying right now, without burying the basket under banners.

What the homepage covers
3
Shop categories up front, each a single tap from the hero: clothing, bags and shoes
8
Products in the filterable favourites grid, with ratings, prices and sale badges
3
Product carousels for this week’s featured items, trending arrivals and best sellers
4
Store promises on every visit: free delivery, returns, member discount and 24/7 support
The challenge

A big catalogue can feel like no choice at all.

Shoppers arrive with very different intent. Some want the new collection, some know the category they need, and some just want to see what everyone else is buying. One homepage has to serve all of them.

Who the homepage has to work for
01Trend shoppers who come for the new season and want to see the collection first.
02Mission shoppers who already know they need a bag or a pair of shoes and want the shortest path there.
03Browsers looking for ideas, who respond to what is popular, trending and well reviewed.
04Returning customers who want deals, their account and their basket close at hand.
The solution

Inspire first, then make every product one tap away.

A clean, editorial look that lets the photography sell, with the homepage moving from campaign to categories to products, and proof and promises closing the page.

01 · Categories

Three doors into the catalogue.

Straight under the campaign hero, clothing, bags and shoes each get a photo tile and a Shop Now button, followed by a scrolling row of the store’s most popular pieces.

  • Category tiles with a single clear action
  • Popular products in a swipeable row
02 · Product grid

Favourites, filterable in one tap.

The favourites grid lets shoppers switch between all products, shoes, clothing and bags without leaving the page, with ratings, prices and sale badges on every card.

  • Category filters above the grid
  • Sale and best-seller badges on product cards
03 · Carousels

Keep the page moving.

This week’s featured items and trending arrivals sit in their own carousels, each with a promotional tile, so the homepage feels fresh every visit without adding more scrolling.

  • Featured and trending carousels with Shop Now buttons
  • Promotional tile for a hero product
04 · Proof & promises

Close with trust.

Customer reviews, styling articles and four store promises (free delivery, returns, member discount and 24/7 support) answer the last questions before checkout.

  • Rotating customer reviews
  • Journal articles that keep shoppers browsing
